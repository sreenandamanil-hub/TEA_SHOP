Indian Tea

Indian tea is one of the most loved beverages in the world. It is prepared from fresh tea leaves grown in famous regions such as Assam, Darjeeling, Nilgiri, and Kerala. Indian tea is known for its rich aroma, pleasant taste, and refreshing flavour. It can be enjoyed at any time of the day and is served both at homes and tea shops.

Preparation

To prepare Indian tea, water is first brought to a boil. Tea leaves are added and allowed to steep for a few minutes to develop their flavour. Depending on the type of tea, milk and sugar may be added. For plain black tea, the brewed tea is simply strained and served. The tea is poured into a cup and enjoyed while hot.

Features

  • Rich and refreshing taste.
  • Prepared from quality Indian tea leaves.
  • Served hot and fresh.
  • Popular throughout India.
  • Available in many varieties such as black tea, milk tea, and green tea.




Coffee

Coffee is one of the most popular beverages enjoyed around the world. It is made from carefully roasted coffee beans that produce a rich aroma and bold flavour. Coffee is served in homes, cafés, and restaurants, and is available in many styles such as espresso, cappuccino, latte, and black coffee.

Preparation

To prepare coffee, roasted coffee beans are ground into a fine powder. Hot water is passed through or mixed with the coffee to extract its rich flavour. Depending on the style, milk, sugar, or milk foam may be added. The coffee is then served hot, while some varieties are prepared and served chilled with ice.

Features

  • Rich aroma and bold flavour.
  • Prepared from roasted coffee beans.
  • Served hot or cold.
  • Popular around the world.
  • Available in many varieties such as espresso, cappuccino, latte, and black coffee.




Black Tea

Black tea is a classic beverage made from fully oxidized tea leaves, giving it a deep colour, rich aroma, and bold flavour. It is one of the most widely consumed types of tea in the world and is enjoyed both plain and with milk. Black tea is commonly produced in regions such as Assam, Darjeeling, and Nilgiri in India.

Preparation

To prepare black tea, water is brought to a boil and tea leaves are added. The tea is allowed to steep for a few minutes until it develops its rich colour and taste. The brewed tea is then strained into a cup and served hot. Sugar or milk may be added according to personal preference.

Features

  • Strong and refreshing flavour.
  • Made from fully oxidized tea leaves.
  • Rich aroma and dark amber colour.
  • Served hot and fresh.
  • Popular in homes and tea shops worldwide.




Black Coffee

Black coffee is a popular beverage made from freshly brewed coffee without milk. It has a bold flavour, rich aroma, and deep dark colour. Black coffee is enjoyed by people around the world and is often chosen for its pure coffee taste and refreshing character.

Features

  • Enjoyed hot and fresh.

Cappuccino

Cappuccino is a classic Italian coffee beverage made with espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of milk foam. It is known for its smooth texture, creamy taste, and rich coffee aroma. The balanced combination of espresso and milk creates a pleasant flavour that is enjoyed by coffee lovers around the world. Cappuccino is commonly served in cafés and coffee shops and is often topped with a light dusting of cocoa powder or chocolate powder for extra flavour and decoration.

Preparation

To prepare cappuccino, freshly ground coffee beans are brewed to make a strong espresso. Fresh milk is steamed until it becomes hot and silky, then frothed to create a thick, creamy foam. The steamed milk is gently poured over the espresso, followed by the milk foam to form distinct layers. The drink is served hot and may be finished with a light sprinkle of cocoa powder or chocolate powder before serving.

Features

  • Rich espresso flavour with creamy milk.
  • Prepared with espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam.
  • Smooth texture and pleasant aroma.
  • Served hot in cafés and coffee shops.
  • Often decorated with cocoa or chocolate powder.




Bubble Tea

Bubble tea, also known as boba tea, is a popular beverage that originated in Taiwan. It is prepared by combining brewed tea, milk or fruit flavours, and chewy tapioca pearls. Bubble tea is known for its smooth texture, refreshing taste, and unique combination of drink and soft pearls. It is available in many flavours and is enjoyed by people of all ages around the world.

Preparation

To prepare bubble tea, tea is first brewed and allowed to cool. Tapioca pearls are cooked until soft and chewy, then placed in a serving cup. Milk, fruit flavouring, or sweetener may be added according to the selected variety. Ice is added for a chilled drink, and the tea is poured over the pearls. The beverage is served with a wide straw that allows the pearls to be enjoyed along with the tea.

Features

  • Smooth and refreshing flavour.
  • Prepared with tea and chewy tapioca pearls.
  • Served chilled with a wide straw.
  • Available in milk tea and fruit tea varieties.
  • Popular in cafés and tea shops worldwide.




Mocha

Mocha is a rich and creamy coffee beverage made by combining espresso, steamed milk, and chocolate. It is known for its smooth texture, balanced coffee flavour, and delicious chocolate taste. Mocha is one of the most popular café drinks, offering the perfect blend of coffee and sweetness. It is served both hot and cold, making it a favourite choice for coffee lovers.

Preparation

To prepare mocha, freshly brewed espresso is combined with chocolate syrup or cocoa. Steamed milk is added and mixed well to create a smooth and creamy drink. The beverage is then topped with milk foam or whipped cream according to the preferred style. Mocha is served hot, while iced versions are prepared with chilled milk and ice cubes.

Features

  • Rich coffee and chocolate flavour.
  • Prepared with espresso, milk, and chocolate.
  • Smooth, creamy texture with a pleasant aroma.
  • Served hot or cold.
  • Popular in cafés and coffee shops worldwide.




Latte

Latte is a smooth and creamy coffee beverage made with espresso and steamed milk. It has a mild coffee flavour, silky texture, and a light layer of milk foam on top. Latte is one of the most popular coffee drinks served in cafés around the world and is enjoyed for its balanced taste and rich aroma. It can be served plain or with flavours such as vanilla, caramel, or hazelnut.

Preparation

To prepare a latte, a shot of freshly brewed espresso is poured into a cup. Steamed milk is slowly added to create a smooth and creamy texture, followed by a thin layer of milk foam. The drink is served hot, while iced lattes are prepared by pouring chilled milk and espresso over ice. Sweeteners or flavoured syrups may be added according to personal preference.

Features

  • Smooth and creamy texture.
  • Prepared with espresso and steamed milk.
  • Light layer of milk foam on top.
  • Served hot or cold.
  • Popular in cafés and coffee shops worldwide.




Fruit Tea

Thai Milk Tea

Thai milk tea is a rich and refreshing beverage that originated in Thailand. It is prepared by combining strong brewed tea with milk and sweetener, giving it a smooth texture and a distinctive orange colour. Thai milk tea has a balanced blend of tea flavour and creamy sweetness, making it one of the most popular milk tea varieties served in cafés and tea shops around the world.

To prepare Thai milk tea, strong black tea is brewed until it develops a rich colour and aroma. The tea is strained and mixed with milk and sweetener until smooth. It may be served hot or poured over ice for a chilled version. The drink is often presented in a tall glass and enjoyed immediately.

Features

Kashmiri Pink Tea

Kashmiri Pink Tea, also known as Noon Chai, is a traditional beverage from the Kashmir region. It is well known for its beautiful pink colour, smooth texture, and rich aroma. This unique tea has a creamy taste and is commonly served during special occasions and family gatherings. It is enjoyed both in homes and cafés for its distinctive appearance and flavour.

Preparation

To prepare Kashmiri Pink Tea, special tea leaves are brewed in water until a rich infusion is formed. Milk is then added and the tea is gently simmered to develop its characteristic pink colour and creamy texture. The tea is stirred well and served hot. Sugar or salt may be added according to the traditional or preferred style.

Features

  • Beautiful natural pink colour.
  • Smooth, creamy texture and rich aroma.
  • Prepared with special tea leaves and milk.
  • Served hot and fresh.
  • A traditional beverage from Kashmir.
